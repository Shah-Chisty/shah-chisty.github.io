About Me

I'm a front-end developer in Reno, Nevada, building websites, web apps, and mobile apps. Since April 2023 I've been at BDG Web Design, working on production sites and applications for multi-location local businesses. Most of my time goes into custom WordPress builds, React and Redux front ends backed by Laravel REST APIs, and cross-platform iOS and Android features using Ionic React and Capacitor. I take projects end to end, from client requirements through launch, and anything that required maintenance or debugging after launch.

Before that I spent 2022 to 2023 at KPS3, a marketing agency here in Reno, building and maintaining commercial WordPress sites on custom themes, including work for a regional international airport. I built reusable ACF components so content teams could easily update their own pages, and spent a lot of time debugging production issues on live sites. That year moved my skills further than any course had.

In 2020 I completed the front-end developer Techdegree at Team Treehouse. Before that, I have finished front-end developer programs at Udacity, Udemy, and Code Academy.
